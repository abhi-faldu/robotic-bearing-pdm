## 2. Business & Thesis

Intuitive is the dominant **robotic-assisted surgery** company: the **da Vinci**
surgical system (now the da Vinci 5 platform) and **Ion** (robotic bronchoscopy for
lung biopsy). The model is **razor/razor-blade**: place systems, then earn high-margin
**recurring revenue** from instruments, accessories, and service that scales with
*procedure volume*. ~80%+ of revenue is recurring.

**Bull thesis:** A wide-moat, installed-base compounder — every placed system creates
an annuity that grows with procedures (+16–17%); da Vinci 5 upgrade cycle drives
placements; Ion opens a large new lung-biopsy market; expanding into new procedure
categories and geographies; pristine balance sheet (net cash), high margins, EBIT +40%.

**Bear thesis:** **Valuation** — perennially a premium multiple that prices in years
of compounding; new competition arriving (see §3); procedure growth could decelerate;
capital-equipment cyclicality in hospital budgets; FX/Asia softness (Q1 showed mixed
Asia).

## 3. Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Status |
|---|---|
| **Medtronic (Hugo)** | Soft-tissue robotic system rolling out — the most-watched challenger |
| **J&J (Ottava)** | In clinical/launch phase |
| **CMR Surgical (Versius)** | International presence |
| Stryker / others | Orthopedic robotics (adjacent, not direct soft-tissue) |

Intuitive's moat is formidable: ~decades installed base, surgeon training/switching
costs, a deep instrument ecosystem, and data. New entrants will take share at the
margin, but displacing the installed base is slow and hard. **Competition is the key
long-term watch item, not a near-term threat.**

## 4. Valuation Considerations

ISRG is the **quality anchor** of the deep-dive list and almost always trades at a
premium P/E and EV/EBITDA reflecting durable ~mid-teens+ growth, ~high margins, and
recurring revenue. The valuation debate is never "is it a good business" but "**is the
premium justified vs. decelerating growth + new competition.**" Some analysts price it
above $700 post-Q1 (`[verify]`). Use forward P/E, EV/EBITDA, and PEG, and stress-test

## 5. Catalysts

- **da Vinci 5** placement ramp and pull-through (instruments per system)
- **Ion** procedure scaling (lung-cancer screening tailwind)
- New procedure indications / FDA clearances; international expansion
- Competitive launches (Medtronic Hugo, J&J Ottava) — share-defense proof points
- Margin trajectory as da Vinci 5 scales

## 6. Key Risks

1. **Valuation / premium multiple** — limited margin of safety.
2. **Competition** — Medtronic/J&J entering soft-tissue robotics.
3. **Procedure-growth deceleration** — the entire model keys off it.
4. **Hospital capital budgets** — placement cyclicality.
5. **FX / regional softness** (Asia mixed in Q1).

## 7. Bottom Line

The highest-quality compounder on the deep-dive list, and Q1 reaffirmed it: 23%
revenue growth, ~17% procedure growth, accelerating da Vinci 5 placements, ~40% EBIT
jump, and a raised outlook. There is essentially **no fundamental debate** — only a
**valuation and competition** debate. It's the "sleep-well" quality anchor against the
high-beta AI/space/crypto names elsewhere in the watchlist, with the trade-off that you
rarely get it cheap.
