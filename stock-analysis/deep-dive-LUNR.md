## 2. Business & Thesis

Intuitive Machines is a lunar/cislunar space company: **lunar landers** (Nova series,
incl. the larger Nova-D), **NASA CLPS** delivery missions, a planned **lunar
data/relay network (Near Space Network)**, and — post-Lanteris — broader space-prime
capabilities across commercial, civil, and **national security** customers.

**Bull thesis:** First-mover commercial lunar lander with real NASA contracts; backlog
tripled to $1.1B with growing national-security mix (more durable than pure NASA);
vertical integration via Lanteris + ground assets builds a recurring-services moat;
guidance to ~$1B revenue signals genuine scale-up.

**Bear thesis:** Mission-execution risk is binary and headline-driving (lander success/
failure moves the stock violently); still GAAP-unprofitable; M&A ($800M Lanteris) adds
integration and **dilution/financing** risk; valuation rests on a future services
business that doesn't fully exist yet; extreme volatility (−12.6% in a day).

2. **Profitability** — still GAAP-unprofitable; path to FCF unproven.
3. **Dilution/financing** — funding growth and M&A.
4. **Concentration / contract timing** — lumpy, government-dependent revenue.
5. **Sector beta** — moves violently with the whole space complex (today: −12.6%).

## 7. Bottom Line
