## 2. Business & Thesis

Micron is one of three global memory makers (with Samsung and SK Hynix), producing
**DRAM**, **NAND**, and critically **HBM (High-Bandwidth Memory)** — the memory stacked
next to every AI accelerator. HBM has transformed memory from a pure commodity into a
partially contracted, capacity-constrained, high-margin product.

**Bull thesis:** AI demand has structurally tightened the memory cycle. HBM is sold
out / contracted ahead, pricing is firm, and Micron's HBM4 is ramping in line with
NVIDIA's Vera Rubin platform. Margins (~75%→81%) and EPS are at record highs with
demand visibility unusual for memory.

**Bear thesis:** Memory is **the most cyclical business in semis.** Records like these
historically mark the part of the cycle where supply catches up. Capex of $25B+
adds future supply; if AI demand normalizes or competitors flood HBM capacity,
margins can mean-revert violently. The snapshot price implies the market may already
capitalize peak earnings.

## 3. Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Position |

| **Samsung** | Largest memory maker; HBM catching up |
| **Micron** | #3 overall but a strong, qualified HBM supplier; tech parity on leading nodes (1-gamma) |

It is effectively a **3-player oligopoly** — disciplined when supply is tight, brutal
when it loosens. HBM qualification with NVIDIA is the key competitive battleground;
Micron being designed into Vera Rubin (HBM4) is a major positive.

## 5. Catalysts

- **HBM4 / HBM4E ramp** and share gains with NVIDIA (Vera Rubin → 2027 roadmap)
- DRAM/NAND pricing trends (the cycle's pulse)
- Capacity/capex commentary from all three players (supply discipline = key)

## 6. Key Risks

1. **Cyclicality** — #1 risk; records can precede downturns.
2. **HBM supply additions** from Samsung/Hynix compressing pricing.
3. **AI-capex dependence** — same factor as NVDA.
4. **Capital intensity** — $25B+ capex; FCF swings with the cycle.
5. **Price-data anomaly** — validate the $989.60 snapshot.

## 7. Bottom Line

Operationally, this is a once-in-a-cycle memory boom — ~$24B revenue, ~75% gross
margin, record guidance. The thesis hinges on one question: **is HBM a structural
re-rating of memory, or a spectacular cyclical peak?** Bulls say AI demand has broken
the old cycle; bears say "it's still memory." Position sizing should respect that this
is the highest-cyclicality name on the deep-dive list. **Verify the snapshot price
