## 2. Business & Thesis

GE Vernova is the power-and-energy spin-out of GE: **Power** (gas turbines, nuclear,
hydro), **Wind** (onshore/offshore), and **Electrification** (grid equipment,
transformers, grid software). It is a direct, "picks-and-shovels" beneficiary of the
data-center power crunch and broad grid build-out.

**Bull thesis:** AI data centers need enormous, reliable baseload power and grid
capacity. Gas turbines and grid equipment are supply-constrained — GEV has pricing
power and a $163B backlog. Electrification is compounding at 60%+ with expanding
margins. FCF inflection funds buybacks/dividends.

**Bear thesis:** Wind remains a margin drag/loss center; the stock has re-rated hugely
(snapshot $942.60 is elevated — `[verify]`), pricing in years of execution; cyclical
exposure to utility/industrial capex; backlog conversion and supply-chain execution risk.
